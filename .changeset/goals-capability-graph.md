Add the read-only goals capability graph: `beep goals next`, `beep goals explain
<slug>`, and `beep goals catalog` decode the whole manifest census fail-closed,
answer frontier, fog, cycle, and unlock-path questions in an in-memory SQLite
engine, and print versioned JSON or human reports. A pure-TypeScript reference
evaluator pins the SQL through a differential test.
