## Public Summary

The line-value locator used an unanchored lazy regex. A repository line with many opening delimiters and no closing delimiter caused repeated scans and quadratic CPU work during a models check.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Verdict: confirmed at current HEAD. Current HEAD used a global before(.*?)after regex for every selected line. The seeded JudgePack locator selects empty-prefix lines, and an adversarial repeated-opening line exercised the slow path.

## Remediation

Scan each line once with literal delimiter searches and advance past each completed match. The focused repeated-opening regression and all locator tests pass.
