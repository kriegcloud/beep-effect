# CSF-052: Future\-dated leases bypass the 30\-day suppression limit

- Severity: Informational
- Codex ID: `commit:d295a567040c819194249ea150b0825f`
- Source commit: `620ebcf28528abdd879d1e1972f858c453871464`
- Owner: Knowledge doctor lease governance
- Status: remediated locally; merge and cloud closure pending

## Public Summary

A lease issued in the future but expiring within 30 days of that future date passed the lifetime check and was treated as effective immediately. It could suppress an introduced knowledge finding for years.

## Current-HEAD Validation

**Confirmed.** The evaluator checked duration and expiry but never compared `issued` with `now`. A test lease issued in 2099 with a 29-day term demonstrates that the old source would classify it active today.

## Remediation

The evaluator emits a dedicated `lease-issued-in-future` governance violation, makes the row ineffective, and excludes it from quota allocation. An end-to-end regression confirms the underlying introduced finding blocks the gate. Reversal: allow future starts only with a separate pending status that cannot suppress before its start instant.
