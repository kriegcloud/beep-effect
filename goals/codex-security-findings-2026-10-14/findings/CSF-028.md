## Public Summary

A symlinked goals root redirected stale-stage enumeration and recursive deletion outside the repository before publication.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Verdict: confirmed at current HEAD. The current sweep followed the goals root when enumerating .staging-* names. The review plan did not bind that directory identity.
