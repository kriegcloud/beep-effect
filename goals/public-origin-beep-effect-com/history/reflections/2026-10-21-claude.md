# Reflection — public-origin-beep-effect-com (2026-10-21, claude)

## Summary

The packet shipped in one PR. beep-effect.com is the single public origin:
`@beep/identity` mints every IRI under `https://beep-effect.com/ns/` from one
exported `IdentityAuthority` literal, the new `@beep/public-site` owns the
reserved apex route table, and the two drift authorities moved into identity
composers. Evidence predicate types moved to `/v2` and the QA judge contract to
2.0.0. The new `retired-iri-authorities` law reports 0 findings over 36,895
tracked files and runs as a cheap-gates lane. The brief's decisions D1–D6 held as
written. Seventeen implementation rulings (I1–I17) cover the places where the
brief could not be executed literally.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** `bun run beep create-package` registered `$PublicSiteId` and synced
  every root config in one step. `beep goals bootstrap` gave a schema-valid packet.
  A scouting workflow (7 read-only agents), an implement-then-adversarial-review
  pipeline over seven disjoint work packages, and a six-lens review panel with two
  refute-by-default skeptics per finding together caught one P0 and five P2s
  before publish. `beep-heavy` kept 19 concurrent package-verify runs off the
  desktop slice.
- **Didn't:** package-verify runs started by parallel lanes armed P0 inbox rows
  for failures caused by other lanes' in-flight edits (unbuilt dists, a formatter
  miss), which then needed attribution.
- **Frustrating:** the pre-commit biome step re-formatted the generated root
  `tsconfig.json`; the cache-baseline step was easy to defer and nearly missed.
- **Wished existed:** a single "I added a root script" checklist command, and a
  law flag that includes untracked files.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Deduplicate the tracked-file helpers between the two retired-* laws.
- `@beep/public-site`: brand `SitePath`, and narrow the `HostedUrl` schema to what
  `hostedUrl` and `labRoute` produce. The type-level rejection of union routes and
  file-route paths is already tested.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** run a whole-diff adversarial review after per-package
  reviews pass. Per-package reviewers each saw their own files green; only the
  cross-cutting panel saw that the root-script change broke the cache gate.
- **HIGH — Critical:** keep one byte-verbatim record of a retired name under
  `history/` and defang it everywhere else. That way a hard-cut gate and a
  faithful decision log can both hold.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** do the serial prep (scaffold, the shared literal,
  new shared composers) before fanning out parallel lanes, so every lane builds
  against the same authority.
- **LOW — Consideration:** append-only capture files need the
  defang-plus-supersession treatment, not a blind host swap. The review panel
  caught two rewrites that would have falsified dated records.
