## Implemented Remediation

Keep an 8192-unit budget shared across dependency traversal, path construction/copying and finding accumulation, with recursive depth capped at 128. Give term-by-path scans a separate invocation-wide allowance of `min(262144, 8192 + 512 * locatedTermCount)` so ordinary multi-parent inputs retain useful findings while scan work has a fixed ceiling. Charge both budgets before work and return explicit `pattern-not-covered` findings under `chain-work-limit` on exhaustion. Preserve the global term set and `antecedent-outside-chain` lookup.

The ten-claim ordinary-input control, dense 25-claim DAG, wide-parent copy bounds and 512-claim scan-ceiling control pass. See [round-one budget repair proof](../research/claim-budget-review-proof.md) for the reproduced compatibility failure, bounded correction and final local coverage.
