# Reflection — exact-optional-keys (2026-10-23, claude)

## Summary

Grounded the operator's claim in Effect v4 source and a runtime probe (it held), grilled eight decisions, then shipped a type-directed codemod (440 sites in 106 files, plus 8 more from base merges), a Biome GritQL law at severity error (EF-40), and doctrine amendments in one PR (#2145). Exceptions are explicit and ledgered: 7 Lexical `exportJSON()` keys and two `Duration.DurationObject` mirrors.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the SchemaParityCodemod engine (dry-run, residue, staged biome format) made the rewrite deterministic and repeatable across base merges; rg line-level reconciliation against the codemod report proved exhaustiveness; the pre-commit Biome hook enforced the new law on a merge before CI did.
- **Didn't:** `yeet publish --push-only` cannot publish an unpublished branch; the cheap-gate fallow:audit cannot distinguish rename drift from new duplication.
- **Frustrating:** cheap-gates take ~10 minutes per attempt, and each red creates a fresh P0 row that the stop hook demands be acked before idling.
- **Wished existed:** a codemod-aware fallow attribution mode; a one-shot "run the schema codemods registered in the repo after merging main" command.
